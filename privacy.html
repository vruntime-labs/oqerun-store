Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This page explains the data handling associated with Oqerun Store, based on the published application code and its intended operation. Hosting providers and third-party services may process technical information under their own policies.

Data handled by the application

  • Wishlist: your wishlist is stored in browser localStorage on this device.
  • Installed-app records: the standalone Store records app metadata in browser storage. When embedded in Oqerun OS, the OS receives the installation request and stores app metadata in its own virtual file system.
  • Catalog and downloads: catalog metadata, icons, release information, and downloads may be requested from GitHub or third-party publisher sites. Those services may receive your IP address and standard request metadata.
  • Digest metadata: the Store can check whether a published SHA-256 value has the expected format, but the current installation bridge does not verify downloaded package bytes against that digest. Treat digest metadata as unverified until a byte-level verification flow is implemented. Do not assume a native .oqpkg package has been downloaded, verified, or extracted by the current bridge.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages may process standard access logs and technical request data.

Local data

Clearing site data can remove your wishlist and installed-app records. This does not necessarily uninstall or delete data belonging to an application you launched or connected separately.

Service providers and external links

Oqerun Store is hosted through GitHub Pages; third-party app pages and downloads may be hosted by independent publishers and GitHub. Links to third-party sites, repositories, application publishers, and services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control those services.

Retention and deletion

Where this application stores information in your browser, you can generally remove it by clearing the relevant site data. This may permanently remove local documents, preferences, or installed-app records. The Store does not provide a server-side account or deletion dashboard. Hosting and third-party providers may retain technical logs under their own policies. Clearing site data does not remove data held by those providers. Do not submit passwords, session cookies, access tokens, or other secrets unless a feature explicitly requires them and you understand the risks.

Children

This project is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information was submitted inappropriately, contact the maintainer using the project’s GitHub repository. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application’s data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, use the contact method listed on the VRuntime Labs GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. A dedicated privacy contact should be published before broad public launch.

This policy is informational and is not a legal opinion. Applicable requirements depend on the operator, audience, jurisdictions, and actual deployment configuration. Have it reviewed for the jurisdictions you serve.
